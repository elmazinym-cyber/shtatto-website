    Our story

    Where the Medina meets good vibes

    Shtatto' is the first concept store of Marrakech's Medina — a restaurant, a juice bar and a rooftop hideout rolled into one, a couple of steps away from the famous Place des Épices.

    Start the morning with a Noss Noss coffee and warm msemen, linger over a bowl of rainbow nachos at noon, then watch the sun go down over the rooftops with a rose lemonade in hand. Our kitchen cooks the classics the Moroccan way — tagines, pastilla, harira — with fresh market ingredients and a little touch of Shtatto.
